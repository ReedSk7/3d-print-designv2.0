# Hardware Library Starter

This is a starter reference, not final engineering authority.

Measured dimensions override placeholders. Actual supplier specs win. Actual measured hardware wins. Printer and material tuning wins over placeholders.

## Clearance Starters

- M3 clearance starter: 3.4 mm
- M4 clearance starter: 4.5 mm
- M5 clearance starter: 5.5 mm
- #6 screw clearance starter: 3.8 mm
- #8 screw clearance starter: 4.4 mm
- #10 screw clearance starter: 5.1 mm
- 1/4 inch bolt clearance starter: 6.8 mm

## Heat-Set Inserts

- Heat-set insert sizes vary by brand and profile.
- Use actual supplier specs when available.
- Measure the real insert before locking dimensions when supplier data is unavailable.
- Add enough surrounding wall for heat and insertion load.

## Countersink And Counterbore

- Countersink angle and head diameter must match the selected screw.
- Counterbore depth must leave enough bottom material.
- Keep screw hole diameter, countersink diameter, and counterbore diameter adjustable.

## Wall, Rib, Fillet, And Chamfer Starters

- General wall thickness default: 2.4 to 3.2 mm for light-duty PETG parts.
- Durable walls often need 3.2 mm or more, depending on load.
- Rib thickness default: 50 to 70 percent of adjacent wall thickness.
- Fillet default: 1.0 to 2.0 mm where it improves stress flow and printability.
- Chamfer default: 0.4 to 1.0 mm for exposed edges and support reduction.

## Magnets

- Common starter sizes: 6x2 mm, 8x3 mm, 10x3 mm.
- Measure the actual magnet batch before finalizing pockets.
- Add retention features or an adhesive plan.

## Bearings And Rods

- Common rod references: 3 mm, 5 mm, 8 mm.
- Bearings and rods need manufacturer-specific fits.
- Measure rod diameter and seat behavior before finalizing interface geometry.

## Fit Defaults

- loose fit: 0.4 to 0.6 mm clearance
- normal slip fit: 0.2 to 0.35 mm clearance
- tight fit: 0.1 to 0.2 mm clearance
- holes usually print undersized relative to nominal CAD
- press fits and snap fits need printer- and material-specific tuning
