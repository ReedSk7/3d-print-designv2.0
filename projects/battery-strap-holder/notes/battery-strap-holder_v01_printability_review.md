# Printability Review

## Project Stem

`battery-strap-holder`

## Version

`v01`

## Printer

FDM printer, 0.4 mm nozzle baseline.

## Material

PETG or ASA preferred. ABS or nylon are acceptable with an appropriate printer setup. Avoid PLA for hot battery compartment use.

## Bed Fit

- intended printer: typical FDM printer
- model extents: 74.1 x 11.0 x 7.0 mm target
- bed size: well under common beds
- status: expected to fit

## Orientation

Print flat on the mounting-ear bottom faces.

## Support Plan

No supports expected in the recommended orientation. The raised bridge creates a functional underside gap; inspect the first print and only add support if the bridge underside sags too much for the strap.

## Brim Plan

Optional small brim if printing ASA/ABS or if bed adhesion is weak.

## Wall Count

4 walls recommended.

## Layer Height

0.2 mm recommended.

## Infill

40-60% infill recommended.

## Overhangs And Bridges

The center bridge spans between end bosses. Use PETG/ASA settings with enough cooling for a clean bridge underside.

## Holes, Inserts, And Hardware

4.8 mm vertical holes are parameterized. Printed holes may need reaming or a small diameter increase after the fit-test.

## Snap Fits Or Flex Features

None.

## Heat Exposure

Moderate to high possible in a boat/battery compartment; PLA is not recommended.

## Outdoor Exposure

Possible humidity and heat exposure; PETG, ASA, ABS, or nylon preferred.

## Screw Loads

Screw load is concentrated at the two rounded bosses. Do not overtighten printed plastic against the battery compartment.

## Protected Interface Impact

Reinforcement is applied to bridge width/thickness and transition overlap without moving the screw holes.

## Risk Rating

Medium

## Review Status

`VERIFIED FOR EXPORT`: geometry exported and passed mesh sanity checks. Physical fit is not yet verified.
