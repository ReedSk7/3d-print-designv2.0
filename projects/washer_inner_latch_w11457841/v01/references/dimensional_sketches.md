### Side View Notes

- Overall latch height target: `27.8 to 28.1`

- Lower engagement block kept intentionally square on the lock-facing side

### Top View Notes

- Pivot bar span is driven by the `28.1 mm` upper fit reference
- Spring tab is offset and not mirrored
- Guide pads sit near the upper body, not on the lower strike nose

## Carrier-Reuse Notes

- The model assumes the insert fits into the original carrier by the pivot/capture bar.
- The carrier side pockets likely snap or retain that bar.
- The guide rails / pads are intended to run inside existing carrier guide surfaces and reduce wobble.

## Spring Interface Notes

- The torsion spring appears to wrap around the horizontal pivot bar.
- One spring leg likely reacts against the carrier.
- The replacement insert therefore keeps a small spring-contact tab near the pivot on the spring side.
