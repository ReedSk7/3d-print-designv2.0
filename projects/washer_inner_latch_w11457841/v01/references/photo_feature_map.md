## Visible Functional Features

### 1. Lock Engagement Face

- Located at the lower end of the insert body.
- Appears to be the main face the washer-side lock catches.
- The visible lower strike width aligns with the user's `19.3 mm` anchor.
- Critical.

### 2. Top Hook / Step Geometry

- Small stepped feature above the main body near the top of the insert.
- Seen in the grid photo and in the underside assembly view.
- Likely combines stop duty and release/return guidance inside the carrier.
- Critical to preserve in both versions.

### 3. Lower Body Section

- Main vertical strike arm below the pivot/capture zone.
- Side profile is not a pure rectangular slab:
  - it narrows through the mid-body
  - it transitions into a stepped lower nose
  - it has a relieved leading face
- Critical for lock engagement and durability.

### 4. Spring Contact Features

- Torsion spring wraps around the horizontal pivot/capture bar.
- One spring leg appears to react against the carrier.
- The other spring leg appears to bear against a small tab on the insert body.
- Critical for Version A.
- Important but simplified for Version B.

### 5. Pivot / Capture Surfaces

- Horizontal crossbar near the top of the insert.
- This is the strongest candidate for the carrier retention/pivot feature.
- The bar ends appear to seat into the intact carrier side pockets.
- Critical.

### 6. Guide Rails / Side Pads

- Small side contact pads near the upper body are visible in the underside view.
- These likely prevent wobble and limit side play inside the round carrier.
- Critical for fit into the reusable carrier.

### 7. Stop Shoulders / Travel Limits

- Upper hook underside and small upper-side stop blocks appear to define travel.
- The insert likely rotates or rocks only through a limited angle.
- Important for spring preload and proper latch presentation.

### 8. Broken Plane Location

- User description places the break approximately halfway down the latch piece.
- That aligns with the visually thinner transition between the upper capture block and lower strike arm.
- This is the highest-risk printed region.

### 9. Asymmetrical Features

- Left-side feature width: `7.6 mm`
- Right-side feature width: `1.9 mm`
- The part should not be mirrored.
- Asymmetry appears intentional and likely ties to carrier guidance and/or stop behavior.
- Critical.

## Feature Importance Split

### Required For Fit Into The Existing Carrier

- Horizontal pivot/capture bar diameter and span
- Guide rail / side pad widths and heights
- Upper hook rear/underside geometry
- Stop shoulders around the upper body
- Spring contact tab location relative to pivot bar

### Required For Washer-Side Lock Engagement

- Lower engagement face width
- Lower step / hook silhouette
- Lower body height from pivot zone to strike face
- Leading bevel / relief that feeds the lid lock correctly

### Lower Importance / Approximation-Allowed Features

- Minor mold draft
- Small cosmetic fillets
- Mold marks, embossed text, and branding
- Internal hollowing exactness where it does not change fit or spring action

## Best-Effort Physical Interpretation

- The intact round carrier is treated as a reusable housing.
- The insert is treated as a spring-loaded moving component with an integrated pivot/capture bar.
- Version A preserves the spring-facing details more closely.
- Version B preserves the external fit and engagement geometry while simplifying upper-body detail for strength.
