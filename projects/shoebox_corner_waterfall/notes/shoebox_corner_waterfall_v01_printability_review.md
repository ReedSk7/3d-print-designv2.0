# Printability Review

## Summary

Modeled as an upright one-piece FDM print with flat base contact, flat back glue surfaces, and raised decorative details sized for a 0.4 mm nozzle.

## Checks

- wall thickness: 4 mm back panels, 6 mm base, decorative details at or above 1.2 mm
- overhangs: no large unsupported overhangs intended; relief details are shallow
- bridges: no long bridge spans intended
- support needs: designed for minimal supports when printed upright on the flat base
- print orientation: upright on flat bottom/base
- bed adhesion: brim recommended if the printer struggles with tall narrow craft pieces
- material: PLA assumed for indoor diorama use
- nozzle: 0.4 mm baseline

## Risks

- Detailed variant has more raised texture; use slower outer walls for cleaner paintable lines.
- Real shoebox cardboard may flex, so glue fit should be checked before final placement.

## Recommendations

- Print the simple version first if speed matters.
- Print the detailed version at 0.16-0.20 mm layer height for better rock and water texture.
- Paint after a dry fit against the shoebox corner.
