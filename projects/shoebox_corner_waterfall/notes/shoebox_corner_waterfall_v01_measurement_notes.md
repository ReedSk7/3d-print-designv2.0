# Measurement Notes

## Source

User-provided design requirements only. No physical shoebox was measured in this environment.

## Dimensions

- requested height: 120 mm
- requested width on each wall side: 70 mm
- requested maximum depth/protrusion: 45 mm
- requested base/splash pool footprint: about 70 mm x 70 mm
- requested minimum wall/detail thickness: 1.2 mm

## Notes

- Interpreted the 45 mm depth as maximum protrusion away from either wall at the visible front while preserving 70 mm contact length along both wall sides.
- Back glue panels are inferred at 4 mm thick.
- Base thickness is inferred at 6 mm.
