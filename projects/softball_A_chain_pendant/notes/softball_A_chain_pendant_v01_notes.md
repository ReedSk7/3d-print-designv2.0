# softball_A_chain_pendant v01 Notes

Task mode: `mesh_cleanup_and_remodel`

## Requirements Summary

- Flat-back softball-style pendant with raised decorative A.
- Large version under 120 mm tall and 90 mm wide.
- Small version about 75 mm tall.
- Strong top bail for chains, keychains, bag clips, or necklaces.
- No supports preferred, print flat face up.
- Multicolor bodies for black, yellow, red, and optional white details.
- Revised from supporting rough Meshy/3MF outputs toward a taller decorative A with lower swash and black outline.

## Assumptions

- The provided images and AI mesh files are visual/supporting references only.
- The A shape is original geometric lettering for printability, not a trademark copy.
- The bail is designed for general chain/keychain use, not rated load-bearing use.
- Default geometry prioritizes first-print success over tiny stitched detail.

## Parameter Highlights

- Large nominal body diameter: 84 mm.
- Large nominal overall height: about 104 mm.
- Small nominal overall height: 75 mm.
- Total raised thickness: about 7.5 mm.
- Minimum stitch width: 1.2 mm.
- Minimum bail wall: 3.2 mm.
- Standard bail opening: at least 10 mm x 6 mm.

## Low-Confidence Zones

- Exact visual match to the reference A is intentionally not attempted.
- Rough mesh distortions are not preserved; the OpenSCAD model uses clean rebuilt geometry.
- Multicolor 3MF color metadata may need slicer-side assignment if OpenSCAD does not export color-managed 3MF reliably.
