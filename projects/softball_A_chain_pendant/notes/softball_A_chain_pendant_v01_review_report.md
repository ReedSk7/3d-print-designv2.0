# softball_A_chain_pendant v01 Review Report

## Scope

Rebuild a clean printable softball-style A chain pendant from visual references and rough AI mesh outputs.

## Protected Features

- Strong top bail with minimum 10 mm x 6 mm internal opening.
- Bail wall at least 3 mm.
- Raised stitches at least 1.2 mm wide.
- Raised A and black outline thick enough for FDM.
- Flat back and no-support print orientation.

## Verification Results

- Large single-color STL: watertight, winding-consistent, one connected component, 88.85 x 104.78 x 7.55 mm.
- Large oversized-bail STL: watertight, winding-consistent, one connected component, 88.85 x 108.88 x 7.55 mm.
- Small single-color STL: watertight, winding-consistent, one connected component, 64.30 x 75.75 x 7.25 mm.
- Small oversized-bail STL: watertight, winding-consistent, one connected component, 64.30 x 78.71 x 7.25 mm.
- Multicolor STL bodies exported and verified watertight.
- 3MF assembly created from aligned black/yellow/red color parts and reloaded with three geometries. White highlights remain an optional separate STL.

## Known Risks

- This is an original decorative A, not an exact team logo.
- 3MF was created, but AMS color assignments should still be checked in Bambu Studio before printing.
- Rough AI mesh proportions are supporting only and may not be suitable as direct dimensional truth.
