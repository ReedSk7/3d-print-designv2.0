# softball_A_chain_pendant v01 Printability Review

## Review Targets

- Print flat on the bed, face up.
- Avoid support material.
- Keep the bail chunky and fused into the medallion.
- Keep raised stitches and A features printable on FDM.

## Design Review

- Flat back is continuous at Z=0.
- Raised color bodies stack upward from the base and do not require overhang support.
- Bail wall is parameterized above 3 mm.
- Standard bail opening is parameterized above 10 mm x 6 mm.
- Stitch width is parameterized at or above 1.2 mm.
- Letter features are much thicker than the 1.5 mm minimum.
- Large verified extents: 88.85 mm wide x 104.78 mm tall x 7.55 mm thick.
- Small verified extents: 64.30 mm wide x 75.75 mm tall x 7.25 mm thick.
- Full single-color large and small STLs are watertight, winding-consistent, and one connected component.
- One connected component confirms the bail is fused into the pendant body in the single-color exports.
