# Kobalt 81798 Socket Organizer v01 Printability Review

## Print Plan

- Process: FDM-first.
- Material: PLA, PLA+, or PETG.
- Orientation: flat on bed, pocket side up.
- Supports: none intended.
- Layer height: 0.20 mm.
- Walls/perimeters: 3 minimum.
- Top/bottom layers: 4 minimum.
- Infill: 15-20%.
- Brim: optional if material or bed adhesion requires it.

## Geometry Review

- Blind circular cups have more than 2.5 mm floor thickness.
- Long rounded channels have more than 2.5 mm floor thickness.
- Pocket mouths use a 0.8 mm chamfer.
- Alignment tabs/sockets are flat side features and do not require support.
- Raised stroke labels are shallow and attached to the top surface.

## Risks

- All socket ODs are estimated and should be measured before final full-tray print.
- The tightest web is 2.50 mm in the dense 1/2DR hex SAE row.
- Open side alignment sockets reduce some standalone module extents but are intentional mating features.

## Result

PASS as a printable v01 draft with low-confidence fit dimensions.
